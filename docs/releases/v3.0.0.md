Sparely 3.0 is the biggest update yet: a new look, purchases that log themselves, on-device AI, and the whole app in French.

## ✨ What's new

**A new look**
- New "rising bars" logo, an animated splash screen, and Sparely's own spruce, mint and gold colors throughout. Prefer your wallpaper's colors? Settings → Appearance.
- Redesigned fingerprint lock screen, cleaner page headers and settings pages.

**Faster logging**
- **Purchase detection** (optional): when Google Wallet, your bank or card app (like PC Financial) or PayPal reports a purchase, Sparely offers to log it in one tap, with **Undo** if you tapped by mistake. Pick the apps, and the payment method for each, in Settings → Notifications → Payment detection.
- **Calculator in amount fields**: type `12.50+8.99`, with + − × ÷ keys under the field.
- **Long-press the app icon** for Log purchase, Record income and Can I afford it?
- **Widget**: a new look, a **+** button to log a purchase, and a real preview in the widget picker.
- **Swipe in History**: left to delete, right to duplicate.

**On-device AI** (optional, supported phones)
- Plain-language summaries and answers on the dashboard, Insights, Budgets, Vaults, Recurring, Can I afford it?, wishes and the health score. It runs entirely on your phone; nothing leaves it.

**Insight and planning**
- **Spending by day** heatmap on Insights.
- History's total now says what it covers when you search or filter.
- Variable bills can be confirmed in the app, credit card bills follow the real statement and due dates, savings accounts get term and notice rules, and wishes get their own detail page.

**Safety**
- **Auto-lock**: with app lock on, Sparely locks again after it's been in the background (Settings → Security → Lock again after).
- **Backups include absolutely everything**, and the dashboard reminds you when you haven't backed up in a while.
- Confirm vibrations (Settings → Appearance → Vibration) and Android's predictive back gesture.

**Everywhere**
- **Complete French translation**, with button labels that fit.
- Notification taps now open the right page.

## ⚠️ Updating from 2.0

This version is signed with Sparely's new release key, so Android won't install it over 2.0. Your data stays only on your phone, so move it over once:

1. In Sparely 2.0: open **Settings**, export a backup and save the file somewhere you can find it.
2. Uninstall Sparely 2.0.
3. Install the APK below.
4. In Sparely 3.0: **Settings → Data & backup**, import the backup and pick the file.

From 3.0 on, every new version installs over the previous one and keeps your data.

## 📥 Install

Download the `.apk` below, open it on your phone and allow the install when Android asks. Needs Android 8.0 or newer.
